Drop a picture in here to use it as a chart's title card.

  macie.jpg     Macie Charts
  oliver.jpg    Violet Stallion's Week of Winners 3.0

The extension does not matter. .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp, .gif and .avif are all
tried in turn, so macie.png works just as well as macie.jpg. What matters is the
name before the dot.

The picture fills the card and pans slowly while you hover over it. If no file
is here the chart falls back to its built-in animation, so nothing breaks either
way, and you can add or remove a picture at any time without touching the code.

Landscape images around 1200x400 look best; anything wider than it is tall is
fine, since the picture is cropped to fill.
